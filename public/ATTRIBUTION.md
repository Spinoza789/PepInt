BodyParts3D represents an adult male reference anatomy based on TARO MRI and anatomical illustration refinements. It is not a complete model of every possible human anatomical structure or variation. This interface is educational and is not a clinical tool.

## Human Reference Atlas assets (included in this application)

Earlier repository revisions included female reference anatomy: Kristen Browne and Heidi Schlehlein, Human Reference Atlas / HuBMAP, *3D Reference Organ Set for Female v1.5* (2023). CC BY 4.0. Geometry adapted for this viewer.

- Source DOI: https://doi.org/10.48539/HBM352.BTSQ.586
- Dataset: https://lod.humanatlas.io/ref-organ/united-female/v1.5
- Original GLB: https://cdn.humanatlas.io/digital-objects/ref-organ/united-female/v1.5/assets/3d-vh-f-united.glb
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Adaptations: translated native meter/Y-up coordinates onto the stage, coincident vertices welded and source normals averaged, geometry simplified with a 0.2% per-structure relative error bound, and normals quantized. Colors and display systems are curated for this interface. All 888 source meshes are represented, with 1,073 source nodes available as selectable individual or compound concepts.

This is a reference assembly with whole-body surface and selected organs, including female reproductive anatomy. Its skeleton and muscle coverage is partial. It is not a complete model of every human structure or a single-person scan. Eight placenta/umbilical structures are classified under Pregnancy reference and hidden by default.

## Adaptations in this retatrutide application

Human Atlas application code © 2026 ashemag, MIT. The bundled MIT notice covers adapted pointer/tap handling and the earlier loader; the current compact renderer is maintained in this project.

The interactive display is a composite of HRA female skin/organ surfaces and registered BodyParts3D male torso muscles, ribs and stomach. `scripts/prepare-anatomy.mjs` curates surfaces to avoid overlapping capsules/segments, merges render groups, and preserves source mesh IDs in `public/anatomy/manifest.json`. The manifest records the affine registration. It is illustrative and not a clinically validated registration or a single-person model. Materials, pores/fibres, colors, crop, cutaways and lighting are generated by this application and are not photographed tissue or evidence of pathology. Original unmodified atlas files are retained locally.

Source revisions: Human Atlas `1c38bf35c254a891200d3cedecfd57abebe83d8d` (BodyParts3D) and `d72b4f6` (HRA). Licences and attribution remain attached to all derived geometry.

## Human Head Atlas assets (included in this application)

The head and brain layer in `public/head/atlas.json` is adapted from [Z-Anatomy](https://github.com/LluisV/Z-Anatomy/tree/PC-Version), derived from BodyParts3D, and distributed under **CC BY-SA 4.0**. The source selection and interactive head atlas were published at [bubblik525/head](https://github.com/bubblik525/head). The accompanying `public/head/manifest.json` preserves source object names, source files, hashes, coordinate transformation and the 708 selected meshes.

The geometry is quantized, transformed into the body's meter/Y-up coordinate frame, and rendered with new materials and controls. These are adaptations; the original source identity and ShareAlike requirement remain attached to the derived head layer. See `public/head/ATTRIBUTION.md` for the complete upstream notices.

## Deposited molecular structures

The bundled mmCIF files are deposited coordinate records from the RCSB Protein Data Bank and remain linked to their original entries:

- `8YWF`: receptor-bound retatrutide / GLP-1R complex, https://www.rcsb.org/structure/8YWF
- `8YW4`: receptor-bound retatrutide / GIPR complex, https://www.rcsb.org/structure/8YW4
- `6LMK`: glucagon / GCGR complex with Gs and a stabilizing nanobody, https://www.rcsb.org/structure/6LMK

Entity and label-chain mappings are verified by `scripts/prepare-structures.mjs`. The application derives the observed-proximity highlight from the coordinates; it does not reconstruct missing residues or claim affinity, receptor dynamics, or retatrutide-specific GCGR binding.
